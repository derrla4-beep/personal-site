Analysis

2026

AvePoint

BU Alternative Investment Group, Pitch Team Analyst · 2nd place

AvePoint sells data management, migration and governance tooling for Microsoft 365. The pitch turned on whether that is a real moat or a rented one: the company’s position is built on top of a platform owned by a much larger company that could, in principle, ship the same functionality natively.

So the diligence I ran was aimed at that question — AvePoint’s position in the Microsoft 365 ecosystem and how durable that position is against the competitive set.

I then built an LBO model projecting revenue growth, EBITDA margin expansion and cash burn through the hold period, and stress-tested the assumptions across multiple scenarios rather than presenting a single base case — because for a business like this the honest output is a range and the conditions that move you through it, not a point estimate. We presented the thesis and valuation to an investor panel and placed 2nd.

The model itself is not posted here. Happy to walk through it.
